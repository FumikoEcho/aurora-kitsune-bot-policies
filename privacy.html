1. Scope and contact

FumikoEcho in Canada operates the official Aurora Kitsune Discord bot. This policy describes this bot’s processing; our website privacy policy covers website accounts, applications and relay infrastructure. Discord and broadcasters operate their own services under their own policies.

2. Data needed for playback

Discord supplies server/channel IDs and basic server information, user IDs, role/permission information, voice membership state and slash-command inputs. We process these to register commands, check permissions, join the requested channel, select a station, change shared volume, prevent abuse and disconnect from empty channels. Gateway state may include Discord-provided member/profile details cached by the SDK; we do not use those details to profile people.

3. What we do not do

The bot is self-deafened and does not record or transcribe member voice conversations. It does not request the privileged Message Content intent, scan message histories, request passwords, sell Discord data, use it for advertising or train AI models with it. Commands do not require a website account. Control responses are normally private to the person invoking the command.

4. Storage and retention

Playback selection, volume, current channel, timing and Discord SDK state are held in runtime memory; playback state is cleared when a session stops and runtime caches are lost when the process exits. We do not maintain a database of individual listening histories or persist command arguments. Private configuration can retain the official owner ID and any configured server/role allowlists or blocklists until the owner changes them. Operational logs may contain server IDs, registration events and technical errors, but do not intentionally contain command inputs, voice audio or tokens. The supplied Docker deployment rotates logs at approximately 1 MB per file with up to three files; this is a size limit, not a fixed retention period. Separate host logs or backups may persist independently.

5. Sharing and public displays

Discord receives application commands and voice playback. Our radio controller receives the bot host’s relay requests—not the individual listener’s Discord identity or IP address. The controller’s listening feed may show station titles and aggregate relay connection counts, not Discord users, servers or channel IDs. Source stations and nodes receive relay requests through our infrastructure. Infrastructure services, Cloudflare and Discord may process connection/security information under their own policies, potentially outside Canada. Discord’s own handling is described in its privacy policy.

6. Choices, requests and safeguards

Stop radio playback, leave the voice channel, restrict command/channel permissions, or remove the application from your server. Removal ends that server’s active session and prevents future bot activity there unless installed again. Removing the bot does not itself erase old operational logs or a host configuration entry. Contact us to request access, correction, deletion or review of processing; provide only necessary identifiers and expect proportionate verification. We will assess lawful exceptions and explain limitations, including provider logs outside our control. Restricted host access, private tokens and limited permissions are safeguards, not a promise of perfect security. Report suspected incidents without sharing credentials; we assess notification obligations under applicable law and Discord’s requirements.

Contact and review

For support, abuse reports, station rights, privacy requests or review of a restriction, contact contact@fumikoecho.ca. Include the relevant server or station ID only if needed. Never send bot tokens, passwords or unrelated personal information.

These policies describe the service; they are not legal advice or a guarantee of compliance. Mandatory legal protections take priority. Qualified legal review is recommended before expanding public use.
